Hi, I'm Anna.

I'm a web designer in the Chicago area, working with small businesses, professional firms, and community organizations.

Running my own business, The Reset Co., taught me the question that shapes every site I build: what do you need visitors to do? Book a call, make a donation, start a search, sign up for a reunion. The design follows from there.

Working with me is simple. You'll deal with me directly, from the first call to launch day. You'll get a written quote before any work begins, and you'll see your site take shape along the way, with everything explained in plain English. And after launch, I'm still here whenever you need a change.
