Hi, I'm Anna.

I've always loved building things from the ground up.

I graduated with a degree in computer science and started my career in tech, where my favorite part was building apps and watching an idea turn into something real. I loved the creative problem-solving, but I quickly realized I didn't want to spend 50 hours a week behind a screen.

That led me to real estate development, where I found the same feeling in a completely different form: starting with a vision, working through every detail, and seeing something tangible come to life. I loved it, but as my life changed, I wanted work with a little more balance.

After getting married and moving to Chicago, I started building something else: a life and a community. I got involved with our church, started a Greek dance program, helped bring back our Greek festival, and launched The Reset Co., my home organizing business. Somewhere along the way, I noticed a common thread. I love creating something where there was nothing before, and I missed the kind of building that first drew me to tech.

So I found my way back to websites. Now I get to combine my technical background, the creativity I've carried through every chapter, and the organization and attention to detail that come naturally to me. I especially love working with small businesses and organizations that have something great to offer but need a website that actually reflects it.

When I'm not building something, you'll usually find me cooking for friends, hosting dinner over a glass of wine, curled up with a good book, or out for a walk with my husband and our dog, Cooper.

To me, a website isn't just something that looks good. It's a place for what you've built to come to life.
